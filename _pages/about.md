I hold a PhD in Mathematics from the Programa de Pós-Graduação em Matemática (PGMAT) at the Universidade Federal do Ceará (UFC), where I was advised by Professor Ederson Braga, and I am an Associate Professor at the Faculdade de Filosofia Dom Aureliano Matos (FAFIDAM), Universidade Estadual do Ceará (UECE).

My research is in Analysis, focusing on the study of quasilinear and fully nonlinear elliptic partial differential equations (PDEs), as well as free boundary problems.

In teaching and outreach, I am dedicated to training new mathematics teachers, contributing to their technical and academic development. I also coordinate the FAFIDAM Mathematics Club, a project in which I try to show how fascinating mathematics can be.

When I am away from the classroom and from mathematical problems, I value time with my family. In my downtime, I enjoy good music, playing the guitar, and a good cup of coffee.
